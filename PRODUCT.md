# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

All hackathon participants. They are arriving to check how close the event is and to feel the energy of the event before it begins.

## Product Purpose

This website is a single-surface countdown experience for the hackathon. Its job is to build anticipation, establish the event identity immediately, and make the remaining time impossible to miss. Success looks like participants landing on the page and instantly understanding that the event is imminent, high-stakes, and technologically ambitious.

## Brand Personality

Futuristic, intense, engineered. The tone should feel cinematic, precise, and technologically advanced.

## Anti-references

Not playful. Not pastel. Not generic SaaS. Not corporate-blue event branding. Avoid cheerful campus-fair aesthetics, rounded startup marketing tropes, and lightweight conference visuals that reduce the sense of tension or technical ambition.

## Design Principles

- Build anticipation first: the countdown is the main event, not a supporting detail.
- Signal technical ambition immediately: the page should feel engineered, not merely decorated.
- Keep the message singular: every element should reinforce time, atmosphere, and event identity.
- Create cinematic tension with restraint: use drama and contrast without collapsing into visual noise.
- Reward quick scanning: participants should understand the page in seconds, even under time pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong contrast, support keyboard-accessible interactions, and respect reduced-motion preferences. The page should remain legible and emotionally effective even when motion is minimized.
